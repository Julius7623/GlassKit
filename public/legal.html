Privacy

GlassKit has no accounts and no ads. We do not store the links you paste, the files we prepare, or what you type into any tool.

Video files are prepared temporarily on our server, offered to you once, and then deleted. Anything left over is removed after a few minutes.

QR codes are created in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Your theme choice and your last few links are saved only in your own browser. You can clear them any time from the Recent list or your browser settings.
