How to use GlassKit

Two small tools, no account needed. Here is how each one works.

GlassGrab

Save a public video from a link.

  1. 1
    Copy the link

    Open the video in Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or X (formerly Twitter), tap Share, then Copy link.

  2. 2
    Paste it

    In GlassGrab, tap Paste (or paste into the box yourself), then tap Get video. This takes a few seconds.

  3. 3
    Choose a format

    Best quality is the sharpest, Most compatible plays on almost any device, and Smallest size saves space. Audio only gives you just the sound.

  4. 4
    Download

    Tap your choice and keep the page open while the file is prepared. When it is ready, the download starts.

Only public videos work, and not every link can be downloaded. If something fails, the message on screen tells you what to try.

GlassQR

Make a QR code that anyone can scan.

  1. 1
    Pick a type

    Link or text for websites and notes, Wi-Fi so friends can join your network, or WhatsApp to open a chat with you.

  2. 2
    Type your details

    The code updates as you type. Nothing is sent anywhere, because it is made on your device.

  3. 3
    Adjust the strength

    Low is the simplest code, Medium suits most uses, and High still scans when the code is dirty or partly covered.

  4. 4
    Save it

    Save PNG gives you an image to share. Save SVG stays sharp at any size, which is best for printing.

Common questions

Why did my link not work?

Private videos, videos that need a login, deleted videos, and pages that are not a single video (like profiles or search results) cannot be downloaded. Open the link in your browser first and check that it plays without signing in. Platforms also change often, so try again later if it used to work.

Why is the first try slow?

The server can go to sleep when nobody has used it for a while. The first request wakes it up, which may take a little longer. After that it is quick.

Are my links or files saved?

No. Video files are prepared temporarily, offered once, then deleted. Your last few links stay only in your own browser under Recent, and you can clear them any time.

Can I add it to my home screen?

Yes. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

What am I allowed to download?

Only content you own, are licensed to use, or may use personally under the law where you live. See Privacy & Terms for the full rules.
